🌍 International Travel Budget Calculator

Planning an international vacation can be difficult because flights, hotels, food, transportation, activities and other expenses can quickly increase the total cost. Our Travel Budget Calculator helps you estimate your complete trip budget before you travel.

Enter your departure country, destination, number of travelers and trip duration. Then add estimated flight, hotel, food, transportation, visa, insurance and activity costs to calculate your total travel budget.

Tip: The calculator provides an estimate. Actual prices can change depending on travel dates, destination, season, hotel, airline and exchange rates.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How much does an international trip cost?

The total cost depends on your destination, flights, hotel, food, transportation, activities and trip duration. Use the calculator above to estimate your budget.

How much money do I need for a 7 day vacation?

A 7-day vacation can cost anywhere from a few hundred dollars to several thousand dollars depending on the destination and travel style.

Does the calculator include hotel expenses?

Yes. Enter your estimated hotel cost per night and the calculator will multiply it by your trip duration.

Can I calculate a trip for multiple people?

Yes. Enter the number of travelers and the calculator will calculate per-person expenses for the group.
