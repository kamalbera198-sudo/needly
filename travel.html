Travel Budget Calculator: Plan Your Trip Expenses

Planning a trip is easier when you understand how much it may cost. Needly's free Travel Budget Calculator helps you estimate your travel expenses before you leave home. Whether you are planning a weekend getaway, a family holiday, a solo adventure or an international trip, you can organize your estimated expenses in one place.

Enter your departure country, destination country, number of travelers and trip duration. You can plan a journey lasting from 1 to 30 days. Add your main transportation cost and select the optional expenses relevant to your trip.

Calculate Your Total Trip Cost

Your travel budget may include flights, accommodation, meals, local transportation, activities, travel insurance and other expenses. This trip cost calculator combines the amounts you enter to estimate the total cost of your journey.

You can also review your estimated travel cost per person, group cost per day and cost per person per day. These figures can help you compare different travel plans before making bookings.

Plan a Travel Budget for Any Destination

Travel expenses depend on your destination, season, accommodation, transportation choices and personal preferences. A short city break may need a different budget from a longer holiday.

Use this vacation budget calculator to compare possible plans. For example, you can estimate the cost of a 7-day vacation, compare it with a 10-day trip, or prepare a budget for a journey of up to 30 days.

Estimate Hotel, Food and Transportation Costs

Accommodation, food and transportation can make up a large part of a travel budget. Enter your estimated hotel cost per night, food cost per person per day and local transportation expenses when applicable.

You can also include activities, attractions, travel insurance, visa fees, shopping, SIM or internet costs, extra luggage, car rental, parking, airport transfers and an emergency buffer.

Travel Budget Calculator for Families and Solo Travelers

Every traveler has different needs. A solo traveler may focus on accommodation and transport, while a family may need to consider expenses for several people.

Enter the number of travelers and select the expense categories that apply. Costs entered per person are multiplied by the traveler count, while group expenses are included as entered.

How to Use This Travel Expense Calculator

  1. Enter your departure country and destination country.
  2. Select the currency you want to use for displaying your estimate.
  3. Enter the number of travelers and trip duration.
  4. Enter the total cost of flights or your main transportation.
  5. Open the optional expenses section and select applicable categories.
  6. Enter your expected costs and select Calculate Travel Budget.
  7. Review your results and download or print your budget report.

Make a Practical Travel Budget

Use realistic estimates based on your own travel research. Check accommodation rates, transportation fares, visa requirements and activity prices before finalizing your budget.

The result is an estimate, not a guaranteed quote. Actual costs may change due to seasonal prices, exchange rates, taxes, availability and unexpected expenses.

Currency note: the selected currency changes the display format only. Enter all expense amounts in the same selected currency. This calculator does not fetch live exchange rates or automatically convert currencies.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a travel budget calculator?

A travel budget calculator estimates trip expenses such as transportation, accommodation, food, activities and other costs.

How do I calculate the total cost of a trip?

Enter your trip details, add the main transportation cost, select optional expenses and calculate your estimated total.

Can I calculate a travel budget for 7, 10 or 30 days?

Yes. You can enter any trip duration from 1 to 30 days. Daily expenses are calculated using the duration you enter.

Can I calculate travel expenses for multiple people?

Yes. Enter the number of travelers. Expenses entered per person are multiplied by the number of travelers where applicable.

Can I include hotel and food expenses?

Yes. Both are optional. Activate the relevant checkbox and enter your estimated hotel cost per night or food cost per person per day.

Does this calculator support international trips?

Yes. You can enter different departure and destination countries. Select a display currency, but enter all expenses using that same currency because automatic exchange-rate conversion is not available.

Is this travel budget calculator free?

Yes. Needly's Travel Budget Calculator is free to use for estimating your travel expenses.

Are the estimated travel costs guaranteed to be accurate?

No. The results depend on your inputs. Actual travel prices may vary by destination, season, provider, taxes and other factors.
