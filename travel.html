Travel Budget Calculator

Planning a vacation can be difficult when you do not know how much money you should set aside. The Needly Travel Budget Calculator helps you estimate your total trip cost before you travel.

You can enter your departure country, destination, number of travelers, number of days, travel date, transportation, accommodation and food expenses. You can also add optional costs such as activities, local transportation, travel insurance, SIM cards, shopping, visa fees and emergency expenses.

How to Calculate a Travel Budget

A simple travel budget starts by adding all major expenses of your trip. These normally include transportation, accommodation and food. Additional costs can then be added for activities, local transportation, insurance, internet, shopping and other expenses.

Total Trip Cost = Transportation + Accommodation + Food + Activities + Local Transport + Insurance + SIM + Shopping + Visa + Other Expenses

The calculator also shows the estimated cost per person and the estimated cost per travel day.

What Should Be Included in a Travel Budget?

✈️ Flights and Main Transportation

Transportation can be one of the largest expenses of an international trip. Enter your estimated total flight or main transportation cost. This can include flights, trains, buses or other major transportation.

🏨 Accommodation

Accommodation includes hotels, hostels, apartments, vacation rentals and other places where you stay during your trip.

For planning purposes, the calculator estimates accommodation using the number of travel nights.

🍔 Food

Food costs depend on the destination and travel style. Enter your estimated food cost per person per day.

🎟️ Activities and Attractions

Museums, theme parks, tours, sightseeing, entertainment and other activities can significantly affect a vacation budget. Add an estimated total if these expenses apply to your trip.

🚆 Local Transportation

Local transportation can include taxis, trains, buses, metro, rental cars and other transportation inside your destination.

🛡️ Travel Insurance

Travel insurance can help cover certain unexpected travel-related expenses depending on the policy. Add your estimated insurance cost to your trip budget.

📱 SIM and Internet

International SIM cards, eSIM plans and mobile internet can be included as an optional travel expense.

🛍️ Shopping

If shopping is part of your vacation plan, add an estimated shopping budget so that your total trip cost is more realistic.

🛂 Visa

Visa fees can vary significantly by country, nationality and visa type. Enter the estimated amount applicable to your trip.

🚨 Emergency Budget

It is often useful to keep some additional money available for unexpected expenses such as medical costs, transportation changes, lost items or other emergencies.

How Much Does a 7-Day Trip Cost?

There is no single price for a 7-day vacation. The cost depends on your departure country, destination, accommodation, transportation, food, activities and travel style.

A budget traveler may spend much less than a luxury traveler. Therefore, using your own estimated expenses is usually more useful than relying on a single average number.

10-Day Vacation Budget

A 10-day vacation normally requires a larger budget than a 7-day trip because accommodation, food and local transportation expenses continue for additional days.

Use the Needly calculator to enter your actual estimated expenses rather than relying on a generic average.

14-Day International Trip Budget

A two-week international trip may require additional planning for accommodation, food, transportation, activities, visa fees, insurance and mobile internet.

International Travel Budget Planning

International travel requires more planning because you may need to consider flights, currency differences, visa fees, travel insurance, international mobile data and local transportation.

For example, someone planning an India-to-Japan trip should consider international flights, Japanese accommodation, food, local trains, attractions, insurance, SIM or eSIM costs and visa-related expenses.

Similarly, a USA-to-Europe trip may require a different budget because flight prices, hotels, food and transportation costs can vary greatly between European destinations.

Budget vs Mid-Range vs Luxury Travel

Travel style has a major impact on your final budget.

  • Budget: Lower-cost accommodation, public transport and affordable food.
  • Mid-range: Comfortable hotels, restaurants and a balanced travel experience.
  • Luxury: Premium hotels, private transportation, fine dining and expensive activities.

Needly provides simple planning estimates for all three travel styles. These are planning estimates rather than live market prices.
