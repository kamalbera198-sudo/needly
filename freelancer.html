It can be useful for freelance writers, designers, developers, consultants, photographers, marketers, virtual assistants and other self-employed professionals.

How to Calculate Your Freelance Hourly Rate

To calculate a freelance hourly rate, first estimate your annual billable hours. Then add your business expenses to your target income and divide the result by your billable hours.

Billable Hours = Working Hours × Working Weeks × (1 − Non-Billable Time)

Freelance Hourly Rate = (Target Income + Business Expenses) ÷ Billable Hours

How Much Should I Charge as a Freelancer?

There is no single freelance hourly rate that works for everyone. Your ideal rate depends on your experience, skills, industry, location, specialization, business expenses and the value you provide to clients.

A beginner freelancer may use a competitive starting rate while building experience and a portfolio. Experienced freelancers with specialized skills may charge higher rates.

Before choosing a price, calculate your minimum sustainable freelance hourly rate and then compare it with rates in your market.

Freelance Hourly Rate Example

Target annual income: $60,000

Working hours: 40 hours per week

Working weeks: 48 weeks per year

Business expenses: $5,000

Non-billable time: 20%

Total working hours = 40 × 48 = 1,920 hours

Billable hours = 1,920 × 80% = 1,536 hours

Total required income = $60,000 + $5,000 = $65,000

Estimated freelance rate = $65,000 ÷ 1,536 ≈ $42.32/hour
