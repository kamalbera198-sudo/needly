What is Needly?

Needly is a simple online calculator website designed to help people make everyday financial and planning decisions more easily.

Our goal is to provide simple, useful and easy-to-understand tools that anyone can use.

Our Tools

Needly provides calculators for savings, travel budgets, car affordability, rent, debt payoff, freelancer rates, electricity costs, wedding budgets and mortgages.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make useful calculations simple, fast and accessible for everyone.
