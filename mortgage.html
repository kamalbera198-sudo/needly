What Is a Mortgage Calculator?

A mortgage calculator helps you estimate how much you may need to pay each month for a home loan. It uses your loan amount, annual interest rate, and loan term to calculate an estimated monthly principal and interest payment.

This can help you compare different loan amounts and terms before making a home-buying decision.

How Does the Mortgage Calculator Work?

The calculator uses three main inputs:

💰 Loan Amount

The amount of money you plan to borrow for your home.

📈 Interest Rate

The annual percentage rate charged on your mortgage.

📅 Loan Term

The number of years you plan to take to repay the loan.

🏡 Monthly Payment

The estimated principal and interest payment each month.

Mortgage Payment Formula

For a standard fixed-rate mortgage, the monthly payment can be estimated using:

M = P × [r(1+r)ⁿ] ÷ [(1+r)ⁿ − 1]

Where:

M = monthly principal and interest payment
P = loan principal
r = monthly interest rate
n = total number of monthly payments

Ways to Reduce Your Mortgage Cost

💵 Larger Down Payment

A larger down payment can reduce the amount you need to borrow.

📉 Compare Interest Rates

Comparing lenders may help you find a more competitive rate.

⏱️ Consider Loan Terms

A shorter term can reduce total interest but may increase your monthly payment.

📊 Check Your Budget

Make sure your estimated mortgage payment fits comfortably within your overall housing budget.

Mortgage Calculator FAQ

What does a mortgage calculator calculate? It estimates your monthly principal and interest payment, total payments, and estimated interest.
Does this include property taxes and insurance? No. This calculator estimates principal and interest only. Property taxes, homeowners insurance, HOA fees, and other costs may need to be added separately.
Is the result exact? No. It is an estimate based on the information entered. Actual mortgage payments can vary depending on the lender, loan terms, fees, taxes, insurance, and other factors.
Can I compare different mortgage terms? Yes. Try different interest rates, loan amounts, and terms to compare estimated monthly payments and total interest.
Can I use this calculator for a home loan? Yes. It can provide a general estimate for a standard fixed-rate mortgage.
