How the Rent Affordability Calculator Works

Needly's Rent Affordability Calculator helps you estimate a recommended monthly rent based on your monthly income.

The calculator uses 30% of your monthly income as a simple starting estimate for recommended rent. It also shows your income after monthly debt payments.

Rent Affordability Formula

Recommended Monthly Rent = Monthly Income × 30%

Income After Debt = Monthly Income − Monthly Debt Payments

This is a general budgeting estimate and not financial advice.

Rent Affordability Example

Suppose your monthly income is $5,000 and your monthly debt payments are $500.

Recommended rent = $5,000 × 30% = $1,500 per month

Income after debt payments = $5,000 − $500 = $4,500

Why Use a Rent Affordability Calculator?

Choosing an affordable rent can help you manage your monthly budget and avoid taking on housing costs that are difficult to maintain.

Before renting a home or apartment, you should also consider utilities, transportation, groceries, insurance, savings, emergency expenses, and other recurring costs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much rent can I afford?

A common budgeting guideline is to keep rent around 30% of monthly income, although the right amount depends on your complete financial situation.

Does this calculator include debt payments?

Yes. You can enter your monthly debt payments to see how much income remains after those payments.

Should I include utilities in my rent budget?

Yes. If utilities are not included in your rent, consider electricity, water, internet, heating, and other housing costs when planning your total monthly housing budget.

Is this Rent Affordability Calculator free?

Yes. Needly's Rent Affordability Calculator is free to use.
