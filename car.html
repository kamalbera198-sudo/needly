How the Car Affordability Calculator Works

This car affordability calculator estimates how much car you may be able to afford based on your monthly income, monthly expenses, down payment, loan interest rate and loan term.

Use it as a car budget calculator to find a comfortable monthly car payment, and as a car price calculator to see the price range of an affordable car before you visit a dealership.

It first finds the money left after your monthly expenses. It then sets aside 20% of that amount as a monthly car payment, works out how large a loan that payment can cover at your interest rate, and adds your down payment.

Car Affordability Formula

Available Income = Monthly Income - Monthly Expenses Monthly Car Budget = Available Income x 20% Loan Amount = Monthly Car Budget x (1 - (1 + r)^-n) / r Estimated Car Price = Loan Amount + Down Payment r = annual interest rate / 12 / 100 n = loan term in months

This is a simplified budgeting estimate and is not personalized financial advice.

Car Affordability Example

Suppose your monthly income is $5,000, your monthly expenses are $2,500, your down payment is $5,000, and you take a 60-month loan at 7% interest.

Available income = $5,000 - $2,500 = $2,500
Monthly car budget = $2,500 x 20% = $500
Loan amount = about $25,250
Estimated car price = $25,250 + $5,000 = about $30,250

The 20/4/10 Rule for Buying a Car

A common guideline for car buying is the 20/4/10 rule: put at least 20% down, finance for no more than 4 years, and keep your total monthly car costs, including loan payment, insurance and fuel, under 10% of your gross income. Use it as a cross-check alongside this calculator.

Why Use a Car Affordability Calculator?

Buying a car is a major financial decision. Estimating your car budget before shopping can help you compare vehicles within a realistic price range, plan a down payment, and avoid a loan that strains your monthly budget.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much car can I afford?

A common guideline is the 20/4/10 rule: 20% down, a loan of no more than 4 years, and total car costs under 10% of gross income. Your real limit also depends on your expenses, other debts and the interest rate you get.

What car can I afford?

Enter your monthly income, monthly expenses and down payment above. The calculator shows the estimated price of the car you can afford, along with the monthly car payment it is based on. Compare that price with the cars you are considering.

How much should I spend on a car based on income?

Under the 20/4/10 rule, total monthly car costs, including loan payment, insurance and fuel, should stay under 10% of your gross monthly income. For example, with $5,000 monthly income that is about $500 per month for everything car-related.

How much car can I afford on my monthly income?

It depends on what is left after your regular expenses, not on income alone. This calculator subtracts your monthly expenses, sets aside 20% of the remainder as a car payment, and turns that payment into a car price using your interest rate and loan term.

How do the interest rate and down payment change how much car I can afford?

A higher interest rate lowers the loan your monthly budget can cover, so the car price goes down. A larger down payment adds directly to the car price you can afford. Change both in the calculator to compare. A longer loan term raises the price you can afford, but you pay more interest overall.

How much car can I afford with bad credit?

Lower credit scores usually mean a higher interest rate. To plan for this, enter a higher rate in the calculator. In the $5,000 income example above, a 15% rate instead of 7% lowers the estimated car price from about $30,250 to about $26,000. Your actual rate depends on your lender.

How much car can I afford with a $50,000 salary?

A $50,000 salary is about $4,170 per month. With around $2,200 in monthly expenses, a $5,000 down payment, 7% interest and a 60-month loan, this calculator gives an estimated car price of about $24,900. Your own expenses and rate will change this.

How much car can I afford with a $60,000 salary?

A $60,000 salary is $5,000 per month. With around $2,700 in monthly expenses and the same loan terms, the estimate is about $28,200.

How much car can I afford with a $5,000 down payment?

A larger down payment lowers the loan you need, so the same monthly budget buys a more expensive car. Enter your down payment above to see the difference.

Does this calculator include insurance and fuel?

No. Insurance, fuel, maintenance, sales tax and registration are not included, so keep room in your budget for them.

Is the result a guaranteed affordable car price?

No. It is a simplified estimate for planning. Your actual lender terms and costs may be different.
