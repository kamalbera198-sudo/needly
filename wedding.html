What Is a Wedding Budget Calculator?

A Wedding Budget Calculator helps you estimate the total cost of your wedding by combining major wedding expenses.

Instead of looking at each expense separately, you can use one estimate to understand how much money your wedding may require.

How Does the Wedding Budget Calculator Work?

The calculator adds your major wedding expenses together. Food costs are calculated based on the number of guests.

👥 Guests

Enter the number of people you expect to attend.

🏛️ Venue

Enter your estimated venue or reception cost.

🍽️ Food

Enter the estimated food cost per guest.

📸 Photography

Include photography and video costs.

🌸 Decoration

Include flowers, decorations, lighting, and related costs.

👗 Clothing

Include wedding clothing and accessories.

Wedding Budget Formula

The estimated wedding budget is calculated as:

Total Wedding Budget = Venue + (Guests × Food Cost Per Guest) + Photography + Decoration + Clothing + Other Expenses

This gives you a simple estimate of your overall wedding spending based on the numbers you enter.

Wedding Budget Example

Guests: 150

Venue: $5,000

Food per guest: $80

Photography: $2,500

Decoration: $3,000

Clothing: $2,000

Other expenses: $1,500


Food cost = 150 × $80 = $12,000

Estimated total wedding budget = $26,000

Wedding Budget Calculator FAQ

What does a wedding budget calculator do? It estimates your total wedding expenses based on the costs you enter.
Does the calculator include food costs? Yes. Food cost is calculated by multiplying the number of guests by the food cost per guest.
Can I use this calculator for a small wedding? Yes. You can enter any number of guests and estimated expenses.
Does this include every possible wedding expense? No. Wedding expenses vary. Use the "Other Wedding Expenses" field for costs that are not listed.
Is the result an exact wedding cost? No. It is an estimate based on the numbers you enter. Actual costs can vary by location, vendor, guest count, date, and services.
Can I change the numbers? Yes. Try different guest counts and expense amounts to compare different wedding budgets.
